Arizona Matsuri

Connecting Japanese Culture and Our Local Community

Arizona Matsuri celebrates and shares Japanese culture, traditions and community—creating a festival where organizations can come together, and connect with the local community.

Our Mission

The Arizona Matsuri is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based on the dedication of volunteers who wish to introduce and educate all who are reached in the essence of traditional and modern Japanese culture.

Our Goal

Our mission is carried out with the goal of promoting mutual understanding and friendships with Japan, Japanese-Americans, and the local community.

Our Vision

The Arizona Matsuri Steering Committee strives to incorporate understandings and friendships within the community while engaging in a fun and family-friendly showcase enlightening our community with the most authentic and relevant aspects of Japan.

Financial figures are planning estimates for presentation purposes. In-kind and personal donations are listed separately from the $22,500 identified cash support.
